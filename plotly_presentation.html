Read the design, too

There are just 10 animals in each group. These measurements illustrate a pattern in this experiment; they are not, by themselves, a broad claim about every animal or dose.

One visual question to keep:
Does the apparent convergence at the highest dose reflect a real difference in response, or ordinary variation in a small sample?

Data: C. I. Bliss (1952), via the R datasets package. Supplement codes: OJ = orange juice; VC = ascorbic acid.
